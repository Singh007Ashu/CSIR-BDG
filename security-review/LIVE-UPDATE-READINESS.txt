NEW FIXES
- Native lab accounts can reach financial-year import API; ownership remains
  enforced server-side and caller-supplied foreign lab IDs are ignored.
- Blank financial amounts are missing values, not silently zero. Actual zero
  and Excel percentage values retain their meanings.
- Annual data import generates a Head notification after successful commit.
- Master TRL/CRL inputs require integer values 1-9; mixed text is rejected.
- Master and annual XLSX parsing moved out of the web process to the bounded
  worker. Limits: 30 sheets, 5,000 rows/sheet, 100 columns/sheet and 200,000 cells;
  15 seconds and 128 MB V8 heap. Large workbooks must be split before import.
- Lab login inputs have associated labels and autocomplete attributes; errors
  are announced. Network/invalid-response errors no longer leave saving stuck.
- Lab login rejects unsafe return paths including javascript URLs.
- Previous upload rollback, version-race, storage and worker fixes included.

VERIFICATION

REMAINING LIMITATIONS

filesystem/network sandboxing, comprehensive contextual injection testing,
full accessibility/load assessment and legacy parser coverage remain open.
Actual MySQL migration/restore and CSIR proxy/antivirus require IT access.
Node/SQLite tests do not certify D1/Workers transaction behaviour. Independent
security and business approval are still required before production release.
